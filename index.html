How I Work

No markup on equipment

Cameras, access points, racks, cable — all quoted at actual retail cost, and I show you the receipts. You approve the full quote before any work begins.

First hour on site is free

I come out, look at the job, take measurements, and give you a written estimate. No charge for the first hour, and no obligation to book.

30-day callback on installs

If something I installed fails within 30 days, I come back and fix it at no charge. On printed parts: if it doesn't fit or work, I make it right.

Cloud-free by default

Standard turnaround is 5-10 business days. 48-hour and 24-hour rush available when I have capacity, priced up front so there are no surprises.
