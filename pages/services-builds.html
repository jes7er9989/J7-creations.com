Custom Builds

Desktops, servers and NAS boxes, specced for what you'll actually run rather than what a store wants to sell. Parts at cost, receipts shown.

What I Build

Desktops and workstations

Gaming rigs, editing machines, CAD workstations, office builds. I spec them around what you actually run, so the money goes where it changes something.

Servers and NAS

Storage that isn't somebody else's cloud. RAID planned properly, the OS installed, shares and users set up, remote access working before I leave.

Upgrades and rescues

A machine that's slow rather than dead, a failed part, a build somebody else started. Often a couple of components rather than a whole new tower.

Parts lists

If you'd rather build it yourself, I'll spec it and you take the list. That's what the spec fee covers, and it's free when I build the machine.

Pricing

The parts list is free when I build it

Working out what to buy is the part that saves you money, and it's most of the value. If I build the machine, that's included — no separate charge, no obligation to go ahead after we've talked.

If you'd rather build it yourself, the spec is $75 and the list is yours to take wherever you like.

How it goes

Tell me what it's for

The games, the software, the file sizes, the budget. What it's for decides everything else, and it's the only question I really need answered.

I spec it

A parts list with what each choice is doing and where I'd spend more or less. Argue with it — it's your money and you'll know things about how you work that I don't.

Parts at cost

You can order them yourself or I'll source them. Either way you pay what they cost and see the receipts.

Built and tested

Assembled, updated, stress tested, and not handed over until it holds up under load. If it fails within 30 days I make it right.
