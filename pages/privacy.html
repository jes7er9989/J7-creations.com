Privacy Policy

What this site collects, why, and who else sees it.

The short version. This is a one-person business. I collect what I need to quote your job and nothing else. I don't sell your information, and there is no mailing list.

What you send me

If you fill in the contact form, I receive whatever you type into it — typically your name, email address, phone number, town, the service you want and a description of the job. Any estimate you generate with one of the calculators is included if you choose to send it. Files you attach come through with it.

The form is delivered by Formspree, which passes the message to my email, and that is where any files you attached stay. A copy of the text also goes into my own job records, so an enquiry becomes a job I can pick back up later instead of an email I have to find again. Ask me to delete yours and I will.

Where the job records are

They run on Cloudflare D1, a database on the same hosting as this site, behind a login that only I can pass. Nobody else has an account, and the pages that read them are not reachable from the public internet without that login.

What ends up in there is the text of your enquiry, the answers you gave the form, any estimate the site worked out for you, your budget and timescale if you told me them, the page you sent it from, and the country your browser reported. If you used the assistant first, the conversation goes in too — see The assistant below. Your IP address is hashed before it is written, so the record can tell repeat submissions apart without the address itself being stored. It is not kept in a form anyone can read, including me.

How long I keep it

It depends on what happens to your enquiry, and the honest answer is different in three cases.

If I decide it is not a job — spam, or a sales pitch, or something I am not the right person for — what you wrote is deleted at that moment, not a month later. All that stays is a one-line note to myself saying what it was.

If it becomes actual work, what you wrote moves onto the job record and the enquiry's own copy is deleted. The job record is then kept as a business record for as long as I am in business, the same as any quote or receipt. If you talked to the assistant first, that conversation is saved with it as a file, so I can look back at what you asked for. That file is kept with your customer record and is not deleted on a timer.

If it is neither — still sitting in my inbox, or something that never turned into work — the text is kept for no more than a month, then cleared automatically. That clear-out runs by itself whenever I open my system, so it does not depend on me remembering.

You can ask me to delete yours sooner at any point, and I will.

Reading and sorting enquiries

When an enquiry arrives, I have Claude, an AI assistant made by Anthropic, read it once. It writes me a short summary, says whether it looks like a new job, an existing customer, a support question or spam, and drafts a reply I might send.

That draft is a suggestion on my screen, and nothing more. No reply is ever sent to you automatically. I read it, change it, and send it myself, or write something else entirely. There is no part of my system that can message you without me doing it.

What is sent to Anthropic for that is the text of your enquiry and the conversation if there was one. It is not used to train anything. Files you attach are never sent — those come to me by email and stay there.

When an enquiry lands, my phone gets a notification. It carries nothing about you — not your name, not a word of what you wrote — only that something arrived. Everything else is behind my login. That is deliberate: it means no part of your enquiry passes through Apple's or Google's notification services.

The calculators

The estimators run entirely in your browser. Measurements, part descriptions and prices are worked out on your own device, and a 3D file you upload is never sent anywhere — it is read locally to measure the model. Nothing from a calculator reaches me unless you press the button that sends it to the contact form.

The assistant

The Ask a question box is an AI assistant, not me. What you type into it is sent to Anthropic, whose Claude model writes the replies. Anthropic handles it under its commercial terms, which don't allow it to train its models on what you send.

Whether I ever see the conversation is up to you, and it comes down to one button. While you are chatting, it stays in your browser tab. Nothing is sent to me, and if you close the tab without sending the form, it is gone and I never had it.

If you do press the button that carries the chat into the contact form and then send that form, the conversation comes with it. The summary the assistant writes goes into the message box where you can read and change it before you send — the rest of the conversation goes as it was typed.

How long it then stays follows the same three cases as any other enquiry, in How long I keep it above. The one worth repeating here: if your enquiry becomes a job, the conversation is saved with your customer record as a file and kept, because it is the record of what you asked for. If it does not become a job, it goes — immediately if I decide it is not for me, within a month otherwise.

To stop one connection running up the bill, your IP address is counted for up to two hours. Please don't type passwords or card numbers into it.

Analytics

The site uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages people actually read. It records things like the pages you view, roughly where in the world you are, and whether you clicked the phone number or submitted the form. It sets cookies to do that. I use it to work out which services people are looking for — not to identify you.

If you would rather not be counted, any browser-level tracker blocking or the Google Analytics opt-out add-on will stop it, and the site works exactly the same either way.

Advertising

I may run Google Ads. If you arrive from an ad, Google records that the visit and any message you send came from it, so I can tell which ads are worth paying for. That measurement is aggregate.

Hosting

The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes the requests your browser makes, including your IP address, in order to serve the pages and block abuse.

What I don't do

  • I don't sell or rent your information to anybody.
  • There is no newsletter and no marketing email list.
  • I don't take card details on this site. Payment is arranged directly, and the site never handles it.
  • There are no accounts for customers, so there is nothing for you to log in to and no password of yours to lose. The job records are gated by my own login, not by one you create.
  • I don't read a conversation you never sent me.
  • I don't use your enquiry to train anybody's AI model.
  • Nothing I have ever replies to you on its own. Every message you get from me, I wrote or approved and sent myself.
  • My notifications carry nothing about you, so no part of your enquiry passes through a notification service.

Children

This is a business site aimed at customers, not children, and I do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Asking me to delete something

Email or call and say so. I'll delete it from both places, the email and the job records, and confirm that I have. You don't need to give a reason. If you want to know what I hold about you first, ask and I'll tell you.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version appears on this page. There is no archive of old versions — what is here is what applies.
